Pusula Privacy Policy

Effective: 15 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-15

Pusula helps parents discover the interests of their 3–12-year-old children through short activities at home and quick observations. The app is for parents; it is not designed to be used by children. This policy explains which data is processed and where.

1. Controller and contact

Erdem Tekin · Email: erdemtekin1995@gmail.com

2. In short

  • What you enter is stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database.
  • An account is optional; in this version accounts and cloud sync are turned off.
  • Photos and your location never leave your phone.
  • No ads, no advertising or tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data.
  • We never label, diagnose or compare your child.

3. Data stored on your phone

DataPurpose
Child's nickname, birth year/month, avatar and favourite themesSuggest age-appropriate activities
Observations, moments and your notesBuild the Compass map and Discovery Report
Photos (optional)Your own record; kept only in the app's folder on the device
Consent records, settings, reminder preferencesRunning the app

This data is not sent to anyone, including us. It is deleted when you delete the app or the child profile inside the app.

4. Device permissions

  • Location (optional): used momentarily to sort nearby free activity places by distance; not stored or sent.
  • Camera / photo picker (optional): to attach a photo to an observation. Picking from the gallery does not request access to your whole library.
  • Notifications (optional): weekly and observation reminders are scheduled on the phone.

5. Operations that use the internet

  • App updates: on launch the app asks Expo's update server (Expo, USA) whether a newer version exists. The request carries none of the content you enter; your IP address, device platform, app version and a random per-install update ID reach the server. This ID is not linked to your name or your device's advertising ID and is only used to deliver the right update.
  • Store services: downloads, updates and (in future) subscriptions are handled by Apple App Store / Google Play under their own policies.

6. Optional features coming later

The following features are turned off in this version. When enabled they only work with your choice, and this policy will be updated first:

  • Account and sync: email or Apple/Google sign-in; data stored on servers in the European Union (Supabase, Frankfurt). Photos and location are still never sent.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: event counts without free text or identity; can be turned off in Settings.
  • Monthly Letter (Premium): with your separate explicit consent, an observation summary with name and birth year removed is sent to an AI service (Anthropic, USA), which does not store it or use it for training.
  • Subscription: purchases via Apple/Google; subscription status verified through RevenueCat.

7. Children's privacy

The app is used by the parent. You don't need to enter your child's real name; a nickname is enough. We do not collect data directly from children.

8. Your rights

Because your data is on your phone, you can view, correct and delete it inside the app. For any request, write to the email above; we reply within 30 days.

9. Changes

When we update this policy we change the effective date; for significant changes we ask for your consent again in the app.
